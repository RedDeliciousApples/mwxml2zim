A steam locomotive is a railway locomotive that produces its power through a steam engine. They were fuelled by burning coal and petroleum products.

History

The first practical locomotive was built by George Stephenson in 1829.Stephenson, G. (1829). Report on the Liverpool and Manchester Railway. The Rocket reached 47 km/h during trials.
